Safe to Send · Ep 22 — AI readiness is about people, not platforms

Most AI-readiness assessments score the platform. Mine scores the people who have to use it.

10 dimensions, weighted, built on ADKAR, OECD and the NIST AI RMF. From awareness and prompting skill to data privacy and "confidence without over-reliance".

I ran three synthetic profiles through it:
→ A careful practitioner
→ A blocked enthusiast: keen, but no approved tool and no support
→ A fast mover with loose guardrails

The fast mover had the highest raw score of the three. They also paste customer data into tools they shouldn't. Raw score 79. Final score: capped at 59.

That cap is the most important design choice in the model. A team that's fast with AI but careless with customer data isn't ready. It's a risk moving quickly.
